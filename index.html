DISCORD SOUND BOARD

  1. The soundboard bot needs to be running on Kevin's computer — if it's not, tiles will only play locally in your own browser instead of in Discord.
  2. Once it's running, type /join in a text channel in Discord while you're in the voice channel you want sound in.
  3. Once the bot has joined, anyone with this page open can click a tile and it'll play for everyone in that voice channel.
    • /play <sound> — Plays a sound in your voice channel (autocompletes as you type).
    • /list — Shows every sound currently available.
    • /refresh — Reloads the sound list from GitHub immediately.
    • /stop — Stops whatever's currently playing.
    • /join — Brings the bot into your voice channel without playing anything.
    • /leave — Disconnects the bot from voice.
